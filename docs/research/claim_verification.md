# Claim verification (2026-11-25)

Scope: seed-ledger claims C8, C9, C10, C11, C14, C15, C16 and C17 (PROJECT_BRIEF Section 4.2), plus four Hugging Face repositories.

Method:
- Papers and blogs were fetched with WebFetch, which returns a model-written digest of the page. Quotes below are the digest's quotes and should be spot-checked against the PDF before they go into the paper.
- Hugging Face repos were checked with `https://huggingface.co/api/models/<repo>` for metadata, with raw `README.md` and `config.json` for content, and with an HTTP range read of the `model.safetensors` header for the tensor list. No weights were downloaded.
- Statuses follow Section 4.2. `OPENED` means the relevant section was read. Numbers that come from authors and have not been reproduced are additionally `SELF-REPORTED`.
